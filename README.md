#### I am pursuing my Master’s at Kansas State University while working remotely for Blue Cross Blue Shield of Kansas on the Self Service Team. I also am married to a third generation farmer and enjoy raising my three girls on a Kansas farm. The following slides will use a story template with fictional characters in which Tressa is trying to convince Caspian to consider diversifying the farm in an effort to increase income and weather natural disasters by being adaptable.

<img width="965" height="544" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/87fc6595-5abd-4594-89d7-e839514131dd" />

#### Tressa and Caspian are a happy couple with an entrepreneurial spirit that are looking for the right place to settle and farm. Caspian grew up on a ranch in OK and Tressa split her childhood between her dad’s farm in KS and her mother’s ranch in NE. Tressa and Caspian cowrite books together. They want to chose a location for the farm that is close to family. They decide to look at 2023 agricultural program payouts to farms from the US Federal Government Department of Agriculture.

<img width="962" height="542" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/313b01ff-d5b0-4d88-b8ac-72a7182f7c70" />

#### We start by getting a visual of the cities that are reflected in our data. The data point correlates to an address of a farmer that participated in 2023 for one of the agricultural programs. In Kansas and Nebraska there seems to be less participation in the west. We presume that this is due to less population in those areas as we know the larger cities are more central and to the east in those states. The color of the dot indicates the disbursement amount with the highest being blue and the lowest being dark orange. The size of the dot indicates the amount requested with small dots being the lower amounts and the large dots being the higher amounts. We also notice that the dots seem to be larger in the west as well. 

<img width="964" height="541" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a0c34046-61dd-4da7-95ed-5706837af085" />

#### Next we take a quick look at the amount requested and the disbursement amounts for the states. The blue represents the amount requested and the orange represents the disbursed amounts. The dollar amount to the right of the bars is the sum of the disbursement amount for that state. We quickly notice that while Ok had the highest disbursement amount; the KS disbursement amount is much higher than the amount requested. Tressa wonders if this could mean that there is more potential for reimbursement in KS.

<img width="965" height="540" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a51606a4-08fc-4a1a-9fa0-86809922856f" />

#### There are a lot of programs to go through. A quick look shows us that overall, quarter 4 had the highest disbursement. This isn’t true for all programs. We see that the Livestock Forage Program had higher disbursement amounts in Q2 for OK and in Q3 for NE and KS. This tells us when those states experienced droughts. This will impact Tressa and Caspian as they plan to raise cattle, sheep and horses.

<img width="965" height="546" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d08650c1-c598-4ae4-9652-e7ab08c24fd8" />

#### Tressa really enjoys the Hobby Farm magazines. She is getting some ideas to diversify farm. Caspian isn’t sold on a dairy or organic farming but may be willing to look at specialty crops and bees. Tressa shows him programs for specialty crops and bees with disbursement amounts.

<img width="965" height="544" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/59939694-b538-417c-a652-9e5f34fe8fa9" />

#### Tressa has just received news that her sister is moving back to Kansas! Tressa and her sister always wanted to raise horses together and Caspian agrees to look more closely at Kansas and filter the data accordingly. They start wondering if some programs are more flooded than others with requests for help. They notice that the Livestock Forage Program was able to help 66 percent while the ELAP, for the bees, was able to help 82 percent.

<img width="963" height="544" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aaef834b-faff-43ee-8509-3c01c9e62283" />

#### Caspian expects angus cattle to be the backbone of the operation and knows that several parts of KS have had droughts the last several years. He looks to see if there is a trend with the Livestock Forage Program. There is, as the year progresses the amount disbursed has tended to increase. He expects this is a program they will participate in.

<img width="962" height="557" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bc7da541-ce0f-47b1-b5d2-2e6dd069d1aa" />

#### Tressa and Caspian look at the programs with highest number of payments, which is indicated by the size of the block and what the average payment for each is, which is the number shown. As some of these are based on county they decide to keep this in mind while searching for the right property.

<img width="962" height="542" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dfebd278-bc76-4409-b323-4b85b29f3cc4" />

#### Tressa has her heart set on diversifying their future farm. She shows Caspian some average amounts that were requested and disbursed for the bee program, specialty crops and she has also found a new interest as well, the tree assistance program!. They notice that the average disbursement amount is higher than the average requested amount for each program. Caspian agrees to the bees and wants to research the specialty crops. He reminds Tressa of their time commitments and the decide to wait on an orchard/vineyard, for now.
